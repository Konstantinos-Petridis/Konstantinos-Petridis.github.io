02 — Power BI case study

UK Train Stations

An interactive Power BI dashboard uncovering revenue patterns, peak travel behaviour and passenger distribution across UK stations.

The problem

Analysing UK railway ticket sales and station usage in Microsoft Power BI, to build an interactive dashboard of revenue patterns, peak travel and passenger distribution.

Key insights

  • Total revenue of £741K from 31,619 tickets between December 2023 and April 2024, at an average of £23.44 — a relatively affordable pricing structure.
  • Advance tickets hold the largest share (42%): many customers book ahead to secure lower prices.
  • Revenue is relatively stable after mid-March as travel patterns normalise.

Recommendations

  • Develop targeted strategies to improve under-performing stations.
  • Promote off-peak tickets to balance passenger distribution and reduce congestion.

How to use

  • Open the file in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
  • Explore the dashboards and visualisations.
  • Use slicers to filter by date, station or ticket type.
  • Navigate the map for sales per station, with extra detail in tooltips.

Data source

Data from the Maven Analytics Data Playground. The data model follows the Guided Project course in Power BI by Data Tutor.

The outcome

An interactive Power BI report that turns raw railway data into actionable insights for station management and ticket pricing.
